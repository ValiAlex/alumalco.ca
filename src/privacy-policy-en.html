Privacy Policy

Alumalco is a Canadian company with its own full fabrication facility in Montréal, specializing in high-end architectural aluminum windows and doors. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share and protect your personal information when you visit alumalco.ca, request a quote for custom aluminum windows and doors, apply for a job, or work with us on your project.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal information in accordance with Quebec’s Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector, Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and other applicable privacy laws.

1. Who is responsible for your personal information?

Alumalco is responsible for the personal information it collects and holds. Our person in charge of the protection of personal information (our Privacy Officer) oversees how we handle your information and answers your questions and requests.

Title: Privacy Officer (person in charge of the protection of personal information)

Email: info@alumalco.ca (subject line: “Privacy”)

Phone: 1 (855) 826-6799

2. What personal information do we collect?

We only collect the personal information we need for the purposes described in this policy. Depending on how you interact with us, this may include:

When you request a quote or contact us

  • Your name, email address and phone number.
  • Your project address or project name, city, product of interest and nearest showroom.
  • Project details you share with us, such as plans, drawings, photos, dimensions and timelines.
  • The content of your messages, emails and calls with our team.

When you become a client

  • Information needed to prepare your quote and contract, order your products, and coordinate delivery, installation and after-sales service, such as your billing details, site address, measurements and product selections.
  • Contact details for the builder, general contractor, architect or designer acting on your behalf, if you involve them in your project.

When you apply for a job

  • Your name, email address, phone number, the position you are applying for, your résumé and any information you include in your message.

When you browse our website

  • Technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device and the pages you visit, recorded by our website hosting and security providers.
  • Your language preference (English or French), saved in your browser. See section 5.

3. How do we use your personal information?

We use your personal information to:

  • respond to your questions and quote requests for aluminum windows and doors;
  • prepare quotes and contracts, and plan measurements, fabrication, delivery and installation;
  • provide after-sales service and honour our product guarantee;
  • keep a record of your project in our customer management system;
  • assess job applications and contact candidates;
  • protect our website and forms against spam, fraud and misuse;
  • maintain and improve our website and services;
  • send you news about our products and projects, only if you have agreed to receive it;
  • meet our legal, accounting and regulatory obligations.

We will not use your information for any other purpose without your consent, unless the law allows or requires it.

4. How do we obtain your consent?

By submitting a form, contacting us or providing your information, you consent to its collection and use for the purposes described in this policy. Where the law requires it, we ask for your express consent, for example before sending you promotional emails.

You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting our Privacy Officer, subject to legal or contractual restrictions. If you do, we may not be able to provide a quote or complete your project.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

Our website does not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels, and we do not use any technology to identify, locate or profile you. We only use the following:

  • Language preference: your choice of English or French is saved in your browser’s local storage so the site displays in your language on your next visit.
  • Display setting: a temporary setting in your browser remembers that you have already seen our introduction animation during your visit. It is deleted when you close the tab or browser.
  • Spam protection: our quote form uses Google reCAPTCHA, which collects information such as your IP address and browser details and sends it to Google to check that a real person is submitting the form. Google’s Privacy Policy and Terms of Service apply.
  • Website statistics: our quote form page uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits without cookies and without identifying you.
  • Fonts: our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address so the fonts can be delivered to your browser.

You can clear or block this information at any time in your browser settings. The website will still work, but it may not remember your language choice.

If we add analytics or advertising tools in the future, we will update this policy and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent before turning them on.

6. Who do we share your information with?

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only when needed, and only the information required:

  • Within Alumalco: with the employees who need it to handle your request or project.
  • Partner companies: in Alberta and British Columbia, we serve clients through partner companies. If your project is located there, we may share your contact and project details with the partner responsible for your region.
  • Your project team: the builder, general contractor, architect or designer acting on your behalf, when you ask us to work with them.
  • Service providers: companies that host our website, forms, email and customer management system, provide IT and security services, or protect our forms against spam. They may use your information only to provide their services to us, and they must protect it.
  • Professional advisors: our accountants, lawyers and insurers, when needed.
  • When the law requires it: when the law requires or allows it, for example to respond to a court order or to protect our rights.
  • Business transactions: if Alumalco is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, subject to an agreement that protects your information.

7. Is your information stored outside Quebec?

Some of our service providers may store or process your information outside Quebec, in other Canadian provinces or in other countries such as the United States. Before communicating personal information outside Quebec, we assess the privacy risks and make sure the information will be adequately protected, including through written agreements. Information held outside Canada may be accessible to the authorities of that country under its laws.

8. How long do we keep your information?

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, or as long as the law requires, for example for accounting, tax and guarantee purposes. When it is no longer needed, we securely destroy or anonymize it.

9. How do we protect your information?

We use administrative, technical and physical safeguards suited to the sensitivity of the information, such as restricted access, password protection and encrypted (HTTPS) connections to our website. Only authorized employees and service providers who need the information can access it.

If a confidentiality incident occurs that presents a risk of serious injury, we will notify the Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada where applicable, and the people affected, as required by law.

10. What are your privacy rights?

Subject to the law, you have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you and know how it is used and shared;
  • ask us to correct information that is inaccurate, incomplete or ambiguous;
  • withdraw your consent to the use or sharing of your information;
  • ask us to stop disseminating your information or to de-index any hyperlink attached to your name, where the law allows it;
  • receive the computerized personal information you provided to us in a structured, commonly used technological format, or have it sent to another organization;
  • file a complaint about how we handle your information.

To exercise these rights, send a written request to our Privacy Officer at info@alumalco.ca. We may ask you to confirm your identity before responding. We will reply within 30 days, free of charge, except where the law allows reasonable fees for transcription or reproduction.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec if you live in Quebec, or the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or your provincial privacy commissioner.

11. Emails and marketing

In accordance with Canada’s Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL), we send promotional emails about our products, projects and events only to people who have agreed to receive them, or where the law otherwise allows it. Every promotional email includes a way to unsubscribe, and you can also ask us to stop by contacting us. We will still send you messages related to your quote, contract or project.

12. Children

Our website and services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

13. Links to other websites

Our website links to other sites and platforms, such as Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook and Houzz. Their own privacy policies apply, and we encourage you to read them.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or in the law. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated. If we make significant changes, we will post a notice on our website.
