Why bi-folding doors

An architectural statement.

01

High performance & functionality

Use it as an internal door to break up rooms, or as an exterior door. Top-quality hardware engineered in Europe and a slender, lightweight frame make opening and closing effortless.

02

Architectural statement

In any space the system makes a stunning first impression — a real wow factor, with minimalist frames that enhance any modern décor and endless customization to fit your home.

03

Maximum transparency & luminosity

Only the best Low-E glazing makes the most of natural light, keeps a steady temperature and protects your valuables from fading — all behind the thinnest possible aluminum profiles.

04

Highest glass-to-frame ratio

Quality aluminum lets us reach the highest glass-to-frame ratio on the market — the clearest, most uninterrupted line of sight available today.

05

Weather-tight gaskets

EPDM rubber gaskets resist ozone, sunlight, severe weather, oxygen, acids and ageing, with outstanding colour stability and high heat resistance.

06

Certified performance

Double, laminated or triple glazed for superior thermal and sound insulation, and certified for air permeability, water tightness, wind resistance, thermal conductivity and noise reduction.

Multiple unique features

A corner that opens with no column.

One of the most impressive elements of our bifold system is the corner opening without a column. Slide the panels away and you are left with an uninterrupted panoramic view unlike any other.

The system also lets you use one leaf as a door without disrupting the others.

Quiet & smooth operation

Engineered to glide in silence.

The bifold is available in multiple threshold options, with the lowest at only 24 mm for easy access for all and maximum convenience in high-traffic areas.

The rolling hinges on the bottom rail are precision-engineered for smooth, near-silent travel across the full span.
