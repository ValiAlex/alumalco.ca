The Alumalco standard

We endeavor to combine our customer's personality and elegance into a high-quality product engineered with the most up-to-date machinery and technology.

Servicing Quebec and Ontario, Alumalco and their partner companies specialize in the fabrication and installation of high quality aluminum windows and doors. Our innovative products are tailored to each of your needs.

We offer a high level of specialized information and technical design support — a team of installers, fabricators, design specialists and operations experts with over 50 years of combined experience in the windows and doors business.

The process

From design to installation.

Four phases, under one roof — from first sketch to final install.

01

Design

We work hand in hand with architects, builders and homeowners — shaping each vision into a bespoke fenestration solution made for the home it belongs to.

02

Fabrication

Deep technical expertise and state-of-the-art machinery converge in our shop to manufacture some of the finest fenestration systems on the market.

03

Quality

Every component, seal and pane is scrutinized, and performance is verified throughout production — so what leaves our floor meets the standard we stake our name on.

04

Installation

Certified, expert installers deliver a precise, flawless fit — and stand behind every project with dependable service long after the work is done.
