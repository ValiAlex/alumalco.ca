About us

Windows and doors, done right.

Servicing Quebec and Ontario, Alumalco and their partner companies specialize in the fabrication and installation of high-quality aluminum windows and doors.

We dedicate ourselves to exceeding our customers’ expectations when it comes to the opening systems for their home. The windows and doors of a house are among the most important elements of a high-quality home — they are the entryway to the outside elements.

Purpose

What drives us.

Our mission

To offer customized, innovative aluminum fenestration systems that greatly improve your home and your quality of life.

Our vision

As industry leaders, our purpose is to keep developing innovative aluminum solutions that respect the environment — with sustainable practices throughout our fabrication, because we believe in the importance of a greener world.

What defines us

Our values.

01

Transparency & integrity

From fabrication to installation, the Alumalco team believes in the importance of integrity. Our track record proves that transparency is the best way to do business — within our organization and with our customers.

02

Teamwork & collaboration

We thrive when we collaborate. Our success is directly tied to our team spirit — every Alumalco team member plays an active and invaluable role in the organization.

03

Passion & dedication

We live and breathe windows and doors. Turning your vision into reality is what drives our passion — it pushes us to create highly functional, unique and premium aluminum solutions for your home.

04

Innovation & sustainability

We stay ahead of the curve thanks to our global mindset. We pioneer the industry with innovative processes and state-of-the-art engineering — and our commitment to sustainable products and practices leads the way.

Expertise

A high level of specialized information and technical design support.

Our team is composed of installers, fabricators, design specialists and operations experts with a combined experience of over 50 years in the windows and doors business. Alumalco’s products are tested and certified for North American (NAFS) and European standards.

Every window and door is double-checked for imperfections, then carefully loaded for shipment. We take pride in our product, just like our customer takes pride in their home.

The product

Personality and elegance, engineered.

We endeavor to combine our customer’s personality and elegance into a high-quality product engineered with the most up-to-date machinery and technology. Our innovative products are tailored to each of your needs.

We dedicate ourselves to supplying only the best aluminum products while offering outstanding customer service.

Installation experience

A quality product deserves quality installation.

Our designers and installation team work hand in hand from the time of conception until project completion, giving you a great experience at every step. We use the latest tools and machinery to ensure quality installation of your doors and windows.

Doors and windows installed by Alumalco stand confidently through every warm summer and frigid winter.

Our process

Carefree products, service and installation.

At Alumalco, we work hard to offer you carefree products, service and installation — from the first research to the final handover.

01

Cutting-edge research

Even before our product line is conceptualized, the best engineering minds in the windows and doors industry are already researching and developing the latest technological advancements.

02

Designed for your needs

At the design stage, Alumalco collaborates with you to ensure every detail you requested has been looked over. Our team supports the technical and engineering aspects, and gladly collaborates with your architects, engineers and project managers throughout the process.

03

Fabrication

Our in-house fabrication process is state of the art. We make sure our tools and materials are up to date with all the latest advancements in the industry to offer you the highest quality products.

04

Quality

Offering top quality every step of the way is at the top of our list. We have strict guidelines for quality control and conduct extensive testing on every single one of our products before they reach your home.

05

Installation

We pride ourselves on our high-quality products — that is why we believe they deserve the best installation. We have an in-house and certified installation crew with all the equipment to execute custom-sized glass, even oversized ones, backed by 20-plus years of experience.

06

Support

Our team of highly trained specialists can help you along the entire process, from inception to installation — knowledgeable sales representatives, the latest technology, quality custom fabrication, and a team of experienced installers.
